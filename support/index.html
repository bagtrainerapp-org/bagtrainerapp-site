Frequently asked questions

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed and managed by the store you bought from. On Android, open the Google Play Store, tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Deleting the app or your BagTrainer account does not cancel a subscription.

How does the free trial work?

Eligible new subscribers can start a 7-day free trial. The app shows the trial and the price before you confirm. If you cancel before the trial ends, you won't be charged.

I already paid. How do I get Pro back on a new phone?

Sign in to the same Google Play or Apple account you subscribed with, then in BagTrainer go to Menu → Subscription and tap Restore Purchases.

How do I back up my workouts and stats?

Go to Menu → Settings and sign in. Your progress is then backed up and can be restored when you sign in on another device. Without signing in, your progress stays only on your phone.

I can't hear the coach callouts.

Turn up your phone's media volume and check that your headphones or Bluetooth speaker are connected. If the callouts still don't play, contact support with your phone model and the workout you were running.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Menu → Settings → Delete Account. If you no longer have the app, use our account deletion page.
