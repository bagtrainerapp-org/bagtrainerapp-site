Delete your account

Permanently remove your BagTrainer account and every workout, streak and achievement backed up to it. This page works whether or not you still have the app installed.

From inside the app

The fastest way, if you still have BagTrainer installed:

1. Open BagTrainer and go to Settings
2. Under Account, tap Delete Account
3. Confirm — your account and cloud backup are erased immediately

Without the app

No app? Enter the email address your account was signed in with and we'll open a pre-filled request for you to send.

We'll open your email app with the request already written.

Email requests are verified and processed within 30 days. In-app deletion is instant.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and its link to Google Sign-In
  • Your workout history backed up to the cloud
  • Your streak, achievement and leaderboard records
  • Your display name and profile info
